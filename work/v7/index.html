BEVERAGE / AI PRODUCT COMMERCIAL 2026

V7.

A cinematic AI-generated product advertisement created for V7 — dynamic product-focused environments, creative camera movement, premium lighting and high-impact visual storytelling.

01 — Overview
ClientV7 Premium Soda
Year2026
ServicesFull production
FormatAI Product Commercial

V7 needed a launch film that would make a new blue soda feel inevitable. We built the campaign around the pour itself — turning a single product moment into a full cinematic environment.

02 — Creative Direction

The creative direction centres on velocity and clarity. A hyper-saturated blue palette, controlled studio lighting and a camera that never stops moving, engineered to make the liquid feel alive.

03 — Visual Approach
DisciplineAI Product Commercial
ServicesAI Product Advertising
Creative Direction

Every frame is product-first. Dynamic environments, refraction on liquid surfaces, and deep contrast give the soda a weight that reads premium at first glance.

04 — Production

Scenes were generated, art-directed and composited in a fully AI-driven pipeline, then graded to a precise cinematic colour language.

05 — Final Result

A high-impact product commercial built for broadcast and social — engineered to stop the scroll and hold attention.
